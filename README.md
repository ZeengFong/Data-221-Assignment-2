The questions are nested in folders based on which auxillary files they require to maintain workspace cleanliness. Ensure your terminal is in the right environment so the local file names are recognized.

Question 1 splits a file containing text into individual tokens for each word longer than one character, cleans punctuation from each word, counts the 10 most frequent words and outputs them with the amount of times they appear.

Question 2 uses the same token splitting logic as question 1, pairs each word with the word that comes after it, counts the frequency of these pairs, and prints the five most frequent pairs with the amount of times they appear.

Question 3 identifies sets of near-duplicate lines by converting text to lowercase and removing whitespace and punctuation, then reports the total count of these matches and displays the first two examples found.

Question 4 filters a student dataset based on engagement metrics like study time and attendance, saves the resulting data to a new file, and prints summary statistics for the filtered group.

Question 5 categorizes students into different bands based on their grades and generates a grouped summary table showing attendance and internet access metrics for each band.

Question 6 classifies areas as high or low crime risk and compares the average unemployment rates between these two groups.

Question 7 utilizes web scraping to extract the title and the introductory paragraph from a specific Wikipedia page if the content of the paragraph is at least 50 characters.

Question 8 scrapes section headings from a webpage while filtering out administrative or reference links and saves the cleaned list of topics to a text file.

Question 9 extracts tabular data from a webpage and processes it into a structured format, accounting for missing values and column counts before exporting to a CSV file.

Question 10 implements a reusable function for searching text files that finds case-insensitive matches to the keyword provided and returns the specific line numbers of where they occur.
